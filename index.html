Where the Conversion
Leaks Are

The single most valuable conversion mechanism on this site is the primary signup CTA, but it is being diluted by competing CTAs and a lack of concrete value proposition, causing visitors to hesitate or leave the funnel. The homepage and pricing page both present multiple competing CTAs (Book a demo, Start for free, Get the desktop app, Contact support) alongside vague messaging like 'AI notetaker that works (magic) in the background', which fails to communicate the concrete outcome and forces users to choose among actions, bleeding conversion value.
